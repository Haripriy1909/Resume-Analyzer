      What is Scanline?

      Scanline is an automated ATS simulation platform designed to audit resumes against enterprise recruitment algorithms. By parsing document metadata, section hierarchies, contact nodes, and JD-matching vectors, Scanline highlights exactly what passes automated filters and what gets flagged.
